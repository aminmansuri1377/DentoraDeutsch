# Original sample learning content

Three short German examples introduce enamel, dentin and pulp. They are newly written demonstrations, not translations or exports of the reference platform's question bank. The basic facts were checked against the American Dental Association's MouthHealthy article:

https://www.mouthhealthy.org/all-topics-a-z/tooth

Each sample block links to this source. The samples do not give clinical treatment instructions. They still require an appropriate editorial/medical review before being presented as a complete professional curriculum. The seed leaves 17 broader specialty catalogs as drafts and does not fabricate completion of the reference content.
